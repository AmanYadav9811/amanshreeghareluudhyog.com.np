Our Story

Journey of Excellence

Founded in 2081 BS, Amanshree Gharelu Udhyog began as a small family business which is first time in Rajbiraj, Saptari, with a vision to provide high-quality paving solutions to our community. What started as a modest operation has grown into a trusted name in the construction industry across Nepal. Our journey has been marked by continuous innovation, unwavering commitment to quality, and a deep understanding of our customers' needs.

From our humble beginnings in Rajbiraj, we've expanded our operations while maintaining our core values of integrity, craftsmanship, and customer satisfaction. Every paver block we manufacture carries the legacy of our founder's vision and the hard work of our dedicated team.

Today, we take pride in being one of the most reliable paver block manufacturers in the region, serving both residential and commercial clients with products that stand the test of time.

Our Vision

Vision & Mission

Vision: To lead the paver block industry with innovative design, exceptional durability, and unmatched customer satisfaction, becoming a household name in every region we serve.

Mission: We are committed to transforming Nepal's infrastructure landscape by providing high-quality, eco-friendly paving solutions. Through continuous innovation, skilled craftsmanship, and ethical business practices, we aim to exceed customer expectations while contributing to sustainable development.

At Amanshree Gharelu Udhyog, we envision a future where every road, pathway, and public space in Nepal benefits from our durable and aesthetically pleasing paving solutions. Our mission drives us to maintain the highest standards in manufacturing while keeping our products accessible to all segments of society.
