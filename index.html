About Us

Trusted Paver Block
Manufacturer in Nepal

Aman Shree Gharelu Udhyog has been the trusted paver block manufacturer in Rajbiraj, delivering high-quality, durable, and stylish paving solutions for residential, commercial, and municipal projects.

Located in Rajbiraj-11, Hanumannagar Road, Jhalahi, our family-owned business specializes in crafting premium interlocking paver blocks, including Zigzag and I-shape designs, available in vibrant red, yellow, and gray to enhance any landscape.

As a leading paver block supplier in Saptari District, we combine expert craftsmanship with affordability—ensuring long-lasting beauty for your property.
